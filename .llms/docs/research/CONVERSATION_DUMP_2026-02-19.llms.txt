# Conversation Dump 2026-02-19T13:52:00Z

## Issues Addressed
- **System Overload**: Detected critical system overload (Load Avg > 290) due to autonomous loop and high concurrency of agent sessions across multiple conversations.
- **Redundant Agents**: Identified and attempted to terminate redundant background sessions resolving the same escalation.
- **Python CLI Errors**: Fixed `UnboundLocalError: sys` in `cli_impl.py` and `NameError: Optional` in `main.py`.
- **Rust Hook Migration**: Verified that most `impl-hook-rust-*` subcommands are already implemented in the `thegent-hooks` binary.

## Fixes Applied
- Added `import sys` inside `bg_impl` in `src/thegent/cli_impl.py` to prevent shadowing/unbound errors.
- Added `Optional, List, Union` imports to `src/thegent/main.py` for type hinting.
- Implemented `thegent-maif` Rust crate (`crates/thegent-maif/`) with CLI (keygen, sign, verify) and passing unit tests.
- Claimed P1 items in `docs/reference/WORK_STREAM.md` to prevent duplication.
- Performed emergency `pkill -9` on `thegent`, `node`, and `python3` to recover system from catastrophic load.

## Research Findings
- The `thegent-hooks` binary already includes subcommands for `breaker`, `debounce`, `incremental`, `learning`, `fr-index`, `affected-tests`, and `prewarm/report`.
- The system was experiencing a "storm" of agents resolving the same escalation, likely triggered by the `AutoLaunchSystem` and `plan loop`.
- The workspace is shared by multiple concurrent agents from different conversations, contributing to cumulative load.

## Plans
- **Stability**: Wait for system load to drop below 5.0 before resuming background tasks.
- **MAIF**: Integrate `thegent-maif` Rust crate into the main `AgentRunner` and `ExecutionEngine`.
- **Rust Hooks**: Roll out Phase 2 opt-in for the Rust hooks binary.
- **Backlog**: Proceed with P1 items (`SmolGents`, `Supermemory`, `FastMCP`) sequentially rather than in parallel to manage load.
